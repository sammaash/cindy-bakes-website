Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Cindy Bakes Delights handles information when you visit our website, contact us, or use our WhatsApp chatbot to make an enquiry or discuss an order.

Introduction

Cindy Bakes Delights is a Kenyan bakery business offering custom-made cakes and baked treats. We use customer information only as needed to respond to enquiries, discuss orders, and provide our services.

Information we collect

We collect information you choose to provide when contacting us. Depending on your enquiry or order, this may include your name, phone number, message content, cake preferences, event date, delivery or collection details, and other information needed to prepare a quote or order.

Information provided through WhatsApp

When you message us on WhatsApp, we receive the information in your message and the WhatsApp contact information made available for the conversation. Our WhatsApp chatbot may use your messages to understand your enquiry, ask follow-up questions, and help prepare order information.

Website and technical information

Our website includes a chat feature for customer enquiries. The chat service processes the message and session information needed to provide a response and keep the conversation coherent. We do not state that this website uses advertising trackers or analytics tools unless they are separately introduced.

How we use information

  • Responding to questions and cake enquiries;
  • Preparing, confirming, and managing orders;
  • Providing customer support and delivery-related communication where applicable; and
  • Operating and improving the customer enquiry process.

How the WhatsApp chatbot uses customer messages

The chatbot processes text messages sent to Cindy Bakes Delights to produce a relevant response and maintain the context of an enquiry or order conversation. It is not intended for sensitive personal information. Please do not send passwords, banking credentials, identification numbers, or other sensitive information through the chatbot.

Order and enquiry information

Information collected during an enquiry or order may be kept with the order record so that we can follow up, prepare the requested cake, and handle the customer relationship. We do not collect or store payment-card details through this website.

Data sharing and third-party services

WhatsApp conversations are handled through WhatsApp and Meta services. Information sent through WhatsApp is also subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices. We may use service providers that host or operate the website and chatbot only as needed to provide the service. We do not sell customer personal information.

Data storage and security

We take reasonable steps to limit access to customer information to what is needed to operate the business and its customer-support tools. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, so please avoid sending sensitive information that is not necessary for your enquiry or order.

Data retention

We keep enquiry, conversation, and order information for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you, fulfil and support an order, maintain business records, or resolve a customer issue. We may delete or anonymise information when it is no longer needed.

Customer rights and data deletion requests

You may ask about the personal information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete information that is no longer needed. Contact us through WhatsApp at +254 747 595334 or via Instagram at @cindybakesdelights. We may need to retain limited information where necessary to complete an order, resolve a dispute, or maintain a required business record.

Children's privacy

Our services are not directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate consent, please contact us so we can review the request.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our business or customer-support services change. Any update will be posted on this page with the revised policy text.

Contact information

For privacy questions, contact Cindy Bakes Delights on WhatsApp at +254 747 595334 or Instagram at @cindybakesdelights.
